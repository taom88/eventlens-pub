# Privacy

The open-source EventLens application has no project-operated telemetry,
analytics endpoint, account system, or shared database. Installing the
software does not send local questions, artifacts, credentials, or reports to
the maintainers.

Configured providers or models may receive prompts, evidence, symbols, or other
content needed for a request. Their privacy terms apply. Review an exported
bundle before sending it to an external agent, avoid personal or confidential
data, and use deployment-specific secrets and access controls.

Anyone hosting EventLens for others is responsible for that deployment's
privacy notice, lawful basis, access controls, retention, deletion, incident
response, and vendor disclosures.
